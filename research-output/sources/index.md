## Failure modes / critiques of internalisation by distillation

| # | Source | URL | Grade | Read | Extracted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Wang et al. 2026 (Tsinghua), "When Context Returns: Toward Robust Internalization in On-Policy Distillation" | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11627 (local) | P | Abstract, setup, Tables 1-3 | "Context-induced degradation": re-adding the prompt to a distilled student often LOWERS accuracy (8/14 configs "Regime A" harmful). Worst: Llama-3.2-3B MedMCQA harm 15.7%; Qwen3-4B-Ins self FrozenLake harm 41.2%. Fix NCA (no-context anchoring, forward-KL consistency regulariser): harm reduced in 12/14. Full-parameter FT lr 5e-6, 100 steps. |
| 10 | Zhu et al. 2026, "The Many Faces of On-Policy Distillation: Pitfalls, Mechanisms, and Fixes" | https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11182 | P | Full (via PDF query) | OPSD works when privileged info is a SHARED latent rule (system prompt, alignment pref.), fails for instance-specific PI (math answers). Student converges to normalised geometric mean of PI-conditioned teachers (Eq. 11). Unnormalised Top-K reverse-KL has biased gradient (+1 term) and collapses; stop-grad / renormalised fixes. 1.7B student garbles output -> SFT warm-up stabilises. Reasoning compression via system prompt needs ~8B model. Qwen3-1.7B safety prompt: OPSD fast early but capped by teacher; GRPO overtakes. |
| 11 | Kim et al. 2026 (Microsoft/KAIST), COLM 2026, "Why Does Self-Distillation (Sometimes) Degrade the Reasoning Capability of LLMs?" | https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24472 | P | Full | Rich teacher context suppresses "epistemic verbalization" (wait/hmm); up to 40% drops (AIME24 on R1-Distill-7B). SFT on solution-guided traces: AIME24 54.79 -> 20.21; unguided: 51.04. Fixed teacher beats EMA teacher (feedback loop). Helps when task coverage is narrow (|D|<=128) and hurts OOD. Causal test: penalising epistemic tokens in GRPO reproduces part of the damage. |
| 12 | Kaur et al. 2026 (Princeton), "Rethinking On-Policy Self-Distillation for Thinking Models" | https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05184 | P | Full | Privileged OPSD degrades 5 thinking models (Qwen3-1.7B avg 0.372 -> 0.308); disabling thinking during training preserves performance (0.591 -> 0.590); instruct models improve (Countdown 0.736->0.865). Vanilla OPD with larger teacher helps; adding gold demo to teacher context reverses. "Fork suppression". Limitation: only math. |
| 13 | Shenfeld et al. 2026 (MIT), "Self-Distillation Enables Continual Learning" (SDFT) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19897 | P | Full | Demo-conditioned model as own teacher, on-policy reverse KL. Qwen2.5-7B: SDFT avg prior-task 64.5-65.4 vs SFT 53.4-60.2; new-task Science 70.2 vs 66.2. Scale: 3B -3.3 vs SFT, 7B +4.0, 14B +6.9 => small models have weak ICL teacher. Artifacts: "Based on the text..." leak; fix = mask first tokens. Cost ~2.5x FLOPs, ~4x wall time vs SFT. Teacher EMA alpha 0.01-0.05; naive student-as-teacher unstable. Struggles to induce CoT. |
| 14 | Davion & Rui 2026 (Stanford), "Privileged Context as Drift in OPSD" | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07842 | P (very recent, 2 authors, low votes) | Abstract/intro | Content of privileged context moves policy more than its source (KL range ratio 5.1x per-token); adapters sharing content cosine 0.571 vs 0.255 sharing source. Qwen2.5-7B only. Treat as suggestive. |
| 15 | Zhang et al. 2026, "Post-Training Leaves Behavioral Shadows on Unrelated Decisions" (Active Taskless Distillation) | https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29233 | P | Full | Same-ancestor student gains capability from single-word teacher outputs on unrelated near-tie prompts (Qwen2.5-1.5B HumanEval+ +5.34 pp over matched control).
