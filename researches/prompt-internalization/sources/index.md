# Source index (prompt internalization / context distillation)

Grades: P = primary (original paper/doc by the method's authors), S = secondary (summary, press, AI-generated digest), T = tertiary (aggregator/blog). Bias notes in-line.
"Saved" = full text stored in this folder. Others were read in full through the alphaXiv PDF tool (page text returned in-session, not stored) unless marked abstract-only/summary-only.
Date of research: 2026-11-08. Almost all 2026 items are arXiv preprints (not or only recently peer reviewed); most report a single training seed.

## A. Foundational

1. Askell et al. 2021, "A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment" (Anthropic). https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00861 . P, read sec 2.1 + App. B.5. Origin of the name "context distillation". Loss = KL(p0(X|C) || p_theta(X)), top-50 logprobs of a 52B prompted teacher stored, 50/50 pretraining text + StackExchange, all sizes (13M-52B) finetuned with KL. Findings: distilled ~ prompted on many evals; small models are HURT by the prompt (Codex/QuixBugs), large unaffected; contractors preferred full-prompted ~53% over distilled (weak evidence of small degradation); benefit over "finetune on the prompt" shrinks once a small prompt is re-added. Caveat: reports "alignment tax" only on Lambada/code; humans MTurk.
2. Choi et al. 2022, "Prompt Injection: Parameterization of Fixed Inputs". https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11349 . P, read fully (saved text not kept; table values recorded in report). T5-base (220M). PING = distill with pseudo-inputs generated from the prompt (teacher gets prompt+pseudo-input, student only pseudo-input), up to 80 steps. PI score = (X_PI - X_noprompt)/(X_prompt - X_noprompt). Table 2: Spider EM 14.5 (no prompt) / 57.9 (prompt) / 36.6 (PING), WSC PI-score 0.985 but RTE 0.081, COPA 0.274. Efficiency claim 280x FLOPs is an extrapolation (OOM baselines). Limitation stated by authors: gap to upper bound.
3. Snell, Klein, Zhong 2022, "Learning by Distilling Context". https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15189 . P, SAVED: sources/snell_ctxdistill_2209.15189.txt . Framework: raw input dist D, teacher template, student template, answer extractor f. Natural-Instructions: student 9.0 -> 34.7 Rouge-L vs teacher 43.4 (11.1x fewer tokens). Spider text-to-SQL (Incoder-6.7B) 4 ex: distill 22.1 vs gradient descent 13.4 vs teacher 27.7. 8-digit addition T5-small 0 -> 94.7 (scratchpad internalised; 8x fewer tokens). "Mixed" input distribution fixes wrong-association 61 -> 16. Uses 100-sample approximation of the KL.
4. OpenAI 2024/25, "Deliberative Alignment" (Guan et al.). https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16339 . P (OpenAI on its own system; no code/data). Industrial-scale context distillation: category-specific spec in prompt -> sample (CoT, answer) from helpful-only model -> judge-filter (min over k judge runs) -> DROP spec -> SFT on (prompt, CoT, answer) + capabilities data -> RL. Fig. 14 ablation: no-safety model given the spec in the system message learns less than SFT-only trained model. Caveat: explicit CoT recites the spec; only o-series; "evidence" is internal evals.

## B. Method variants (prompt -> weights)

5. Bhargava et al. 2024 "Prompt Baking". https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13697 . P, NOW READ IN FULL TEXT (round 2; round-1 numbers from the AI digest were confirmed). Extra facts: Fig 4/5 are averages of 3 seeds; baked models still write CoT (the user turn keeps 'reason and give a final answer'; only the few-shot examples are baked); the top-p/top-1 logit ablation (Fig 8) is in the paper; 'surpass Meta's published 79.6 on GSM8K' compares against a published number, not a re-run; knowledge baking test = 20 hand-written questions (baked 77.5% vs prompted 80.0%); 'no overfitting' is asserted, not tested; Llama-3 Instruct (size not stated on pages read). ORIGINAL ENTRY: KL(P_theta(.|u) || P_theta_u(.)) with LoRA, trajectories from prompted model with intermediate seq x0 (SQuAD questions), "half-baking", "Prompt Pursuit" (iterate). Claims: within 8% of prompted on instruction tasks; few-shot CoT baking within 1.4% of few-shot prompted on GSM8K/ASDiv/MBPP/ARC/CSQA; re-prompting baked model beat the original (GSM8K 81.0 vs Llama3 79.6) - CONFLICTS with source 11; <=3.4% drop on unrelated tasks. Needs primary check.
6. Shin et al. 2024-25 (KAIST/MSR), "Generative Prompt Internalization (GenPI)". https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15927 . P, NOW READ IN FULL TEXT (v3). CORRECTION: PG loss adds +8.8 points absolute (35.64 -> 44.46, ~+25% relative) on WebShop, not 25 points. Table 1 (Llama-3-8B-Instruct, n=144/100/200 test items, single run): upper bound 17.36 / 17 / 54.16; GenPI 17.36 / 14 / 44.46; SeqKD+KLD 4.86/15/40.26; KLD-only 0/0/0; prepend-prompt-100% 0/0/0; 50% 13.19/12/31.09. QLoRA r16, lr 1e-4, 10 epochs, bs 32, lambda 0.7 (tuned over 0.3/0.5/0.7), 1000 synthetic inputs. Fine-tuned-on-pseudo-data model with prompt is WORSE than original with prompt (16.6/17/51.12). Efficiency measured on ONE example (>5 turns, >1200-token prompt). ORIGINAL ENTRY: Loss = SFT on teacher outputs + "prompt generation" loss (model must emit prompt + reason for AS-IS->TO-BE change). Llama-3-8B-Instruct, QLoRA, 1000 synthetic samples, AgentBench: OS 100% retention; WebShop 82.1; KLD-only 0%; SFT-only weaker (PG adds ~25 pts WebShop); LLMLingua-2 near 0 at 30% rate. Self role-play data synthesis. 39% MAC/FLOP and 17% latency saving even with KV cache.
7. Zou et al. 2024 (Microsoft), "PromptIntern". https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02211 . P, read. Progressive schedule: template compression rate 1->0 and few-shot count 5->2->0 during fine-tuning (cross-entropy on gold labels, LoRA r32). >90% input-token cut, 4.2x speed, 88.3% cost cut; Table 2: still below full-prompt (MBPP GPT-3.5 76.6 vs 82.7; Llama2-7B NL2F 40.8 vs 45.4; smaller model loses more without template: -21.2% Llama2-7B vs -5.7% 13B). Needs gold labels, unlike pure self-distillation.
8. Kujanpaa et al. 2024/25, "Efficient Knowledge Injection in LLMs via Self-Distillation" (prompt distillation, PD). https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14964 . P, read. Self-distil: teacher=student+document in prompt, student LoRA (rank 1024 for 3B/8B), forward KL on full logits, 30 self-generated questions per doc at T=1.5, answers T=1.5. Squadshifts closed-book PD 79.5-94.4 vs SFT 69.8-89.5 vs RAG ~78.6-96.3 (Llama-3-8B). Self-teacher beat 70B/72B teachers (style mismatch) EXCEPT Qwen2.5-3B, which generated poor data. Regulariser: KL to original model on Tulu-3 prompts (no context) keeps MMLU-Pro within ~1 pt.
9. Prompt Baking / PD / GenPI / PI all treated in "Lacuna", "emergentmind" aggregators (T, not used for facts).
10. Jha et al. 2026 (EMNLP main, Amazon), "PRISM". https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.2431.pdf . P, NOW READ IN FULL TEXT. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (4-bit QLoRA, r16), 500-item test sets, prompts 7K-23K tokens. Recovery = (LoRA - prompt-without-component)/(best prompted config - prompt-without-component). >100% arises partly because the full S+E+I prompt is WORSE than E alone on MultiWOZ (12.8 vs 18.8 JGA: long-context interference) and silver data is FILTERED (CUAD keeps 38% by >=80% word overlap with passage; GSM8K keeps 84% by gold-answer correctness; MultiWOZ 99% format check) - so 'label-free' is only partly true. Silver = 94% of gold on CUAD, 80-93% on MultiWOZ. Plateau at ~400 silver examples (CUAD 200 ex = 85%). Latency: 4.4x only on MultiWOZ (examples = ~75% of prompt); ~1.1x CUAD; none GSM8K. GSM8K example-LoRA 79.6 (29% recovery), instruction-LoRA 77.6 (39%). Runtime-instruction compliance 89.5 (LoRA) vs 75.4 (recency) vs 1.7 (buried mid-prompt), IFEval n=541. Beats KL context distillation 37.8 vs 10.4 F1 'under matched hyperparameters' (weak baseline; see GenPI KLD-only = 0). Single run; authors state 3 tasks, 7B only. ORIGINAL ENTRY: Two-inference diagnostic (instructions-only vs examples-only) tells whether to compress demonstrations ("pattern-dominant": recovers 131% CUAD, 151% MultiWOZ of example benefit) or instructions ("procedure-dominant": 39% on GSM8K); -72..79% tokens; silver data 80-94% of gold.

## C. On-policy / self-distillation (2026 wave)

11. Ye, Dong, Wu, Huang, Wei (Microsoft Research) 2026, "On-Policy Context Distillation (OPCD)". https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12275 . P, SAVED: sources/opcd_2602.12275.txt . Student samples WITHOUT context; loss = token reverse KL to context-conditioned teacher (top-k approx); teacher frozen (default) or self. System-prompt distillation (MetaSPO-optimised prompts; Llama-3.2-3B, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-3B/7B); 50 steps, batch 128, max 512 tokens. Medical 3B: base 59.4, in-context 66.4, off-policy CD 71.0, OPCD 76.3; Safety Llama-3.2-3B: 30.7 / 69.5 / 83.3 / 83.1 (OPCD NOT best here). Frozen teacher beats self-teacher (Sokoban 53.9 vs 18.8; self can diverge). Injecting teacher's experience into 1.7B/4B context HURT. Code aka.ms/opcd-code (VentureBeat says verl-based, release pending).
12. Shenfeld, Damani, Hubotter, Agrawal 2026, "Self-Distillation Enables Continual Learning (SDFT)". https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19897 . P, read. Demonstration-conditioned self-teacher, reverse KL, TRL, full fine-tuning on one H200, ~2.5x FLOPs / ~4x wall-clock vs SFT; knowledge task 89% vs SFT 80%; scale: gap vs SFT -3.3 (3B), +4.0 (7B), +6.9 (14B) -> 3B in-context ability too weak; EMA teacher best; learned artifacts ("Based on the text...") fixed by masking first tokens of loss.
13. Zhao et al. 2026 OPSD (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18734) P, NOW READ (v3): teacher = frozen initial policy given the gold solution; Qwen3 1.7B/4B/8B (LoRA r64), 30K OpenThoughts math; avg over 4 math benchmarks 8B 50.0->52.2, 4B 48.3->50.4, 1.7B 28.8->30.0 (GRPO 30.5; HMMT25 DROPS 25.4->25.0); best-checkpoint-within-1500-steps reporting; authors' own note: student prompt lacked chat template while teacher used thinking template (unintentional mismatch, results correspond to it); full-vocabulary JSD beats sampled-token reverse-KL (84.1 vs 82.1 AIME25 pass@8). Hubotter et al. SDPO (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20802): still only via citations.
14. Wang, Chen, Li, Chen, Huang (Tsinghua) 2026, "When Context Returns". https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11627 . P, SAVED: sources/when_context_returns_2606.11627.txt . 14 configs. Reintroducing the internalised prompt to the distilled student often DEGRADES it (Regime A in 8/14); e.g. Llama-3.2-3B MedMCQA harm 15.7%, FrozenLake self-distilled harm 41.2%, cross-model Sokoban 35.7%. Fix NCA: forward-KL consistency between student(x,c) and stop-grad student(x); avg harm -7.2 pts, harm down in 12/14. Limitation: compute-limited, small scale.
15. Zhu et al. 2026 "The Many Faces of On-Policy Distillation". https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11182 . P, read. OPSD works when privileged info is a SHARED LATENT RULE (system prompt, alignment) and fails when instance-specific (student converges to geometric mean of PI-conditioned teachers). Failure mechanisms: student-prefix distorts teacher (Qwen3-14B GPQA 62.1 -> 46.0), biased TopK reverse-KL (collapses; stop-gradient/renormalised fix), PI-marginalisation. Reasoning compression by system-prompt OPSD needs ~8B-scale; safety Qwen3-1.7B worked early but capped by teacher. Fixes: RLVR-adapted teacher, SFT warm-up (1.7B-Base emitted garbage), thinking-mode hacking.
16. Kaur et al. 2026 (Princeton) "Rethinking On-Policy Self-Distillation for Thinking Models". https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05184 . P, read. Privileged-context self-distillation degrades 5 thinking models on AIME/HMMT (up to -17% rel.), worse with more privileged context; helps instruct models (+4 pts). Disable thinking during training preserved base (0.591 -> 0.590). Mechanism "fork suppression" (correlational, authors admit not causal).
17. Kim et al. 2026 (MSR/KAIST, COLM 2026) "Why Does Self-Distillation (Sometimes) Degrade Reasoning". https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24472 . P, read. Richer teacher context -> fewer "wait/hmm" tokens, shorter; SFT on solution-guided (correct!) traces: AIME24 54.8 -> 20.2; harmful when task coverage is broad (|D|>=512), harmless/beneficial when narrow.
18. Yu et al. 2026 (Columbia/AWS) "Instruct, Not Answer". https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32201 . P, read. Short instruction privileges that target observed student errors beat gold-answer privileges OOD (7/8 cases, +4 to +17 pts) and match in-domain; in-prompt instructions barely helped or hurt the same 1.7B model, yet were internalised well by OPCD. Single training seed; autoformalization only. Gold hurts OOD (e.g. -14.2 pass4 PW->PR).
19. Ichihara et al. 2026 "Privileged Solutions or Context-Induced Teacher Behavior? (OP2SD)". https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09228 . P, read. Teacher shown a solution to a DIFFERENT problem still yields most OPSD gains (Qwen3-4B AIME24 23.2 -> 31.5 vs OPSD 30.8); physics examples fail (18.7). Implication: part of the "internalised" signal is teacher-behaviour shift not information. Single run per condition.
20. Davion & Rui 2026 "Privileged Context as Drift in OPSD". https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07842 . P, only abstract/first page read: content changes KL/parameter drift 5.1x more than source does.
21. Ye et al. / Microsoft; Thinking Machines (Lu 2025) "On-Policy Distillation". https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation/ . P (company blog; commercial interest in Tinker). Qwen3-8B personalisation: internal-QA 18 -> 43 after mid-train but IF-eval 85 -> 45 (79 at 70/30 mix); on-policy distillation from the earlier model restores IF-eval 83 with QA 41. Cost claims: 9-30x vs SFT/RL (their FLOPs accounting). NOT a system-prompt distillation experiment.
22. Tinker docs, "Prompt Distillation" tutorial. https://tinker-docs.thinkingmachines.ai/tutorials/advanced/prompt-distillation/ . P (vendor tutorial). Qwen3.5-4B (no-think), 1,572-char language-ID system prompt, 12 sentences, LoRA r32, lr 2e-4, 10 steps, CE on teacher greedy labels; teacher 10/12, student 10/12 on 12 new sentences, SAME two errors copied. Production recipe: 2,100 multilingual sentences, 4 epochs. Toy: n=12.
23. Sakana AI 2026 Doc-to-LoRA (https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15902 , https://sakana.ai/doc-to-lora/) and Text-to-LoRA. S/abstract-level only (via search). Hypernetwork approximates context distillation in one forward pass. Not read in full.

Others seen in listings but not opened: Learning from Interaction Experience via Localized Context Distillation (2610.21427), Updating Parametric Knowledge with Context Distillation Retains Post-Training Capabilities (2602.16093), Transmuting prompts into weights (2510.08734), Can Gradient Descent Simulate Prompting (2506.20989; read as AI digest only: Llama-3.2-1B, meta-learning, recovers a fraction of gap), Prompt-Level Distillation (2602.21103, non-parametric alternative), What Should a Self-Teacher See (2609.25623), Privileged Information effectiveness (2610.22512).

## E. Added in round 2 (2026-11-08, all read as full text through alphaXiv unless stated)

28. Mazzawi et al. (Google Research) 2026, "Transmuting prompts into weights" (thought patching). https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08734 . P. Gemma-3-1B-it, closed-form layer-wise least-squares weight patch (no gradient descent through the loss) aligning no-context activations to with-context activations. 10 examples: sum/multiply 3 single-digit numbers 100% (5 seeds, trivial tasks); ParaDetox toxicity 90.5% -> 6.2% vs 6.7% with prompt; dictionary lookup 100% (k<=8) down to 91% at k=32. Authors: prone to overfitting prompt structure. Only evidence at ~1B for fixed-instruction internalisation, but toy tasks, single group, no code check.
29. Zhang, Choshen, Andreas (MIT) 2025, "Can Gradient Descent Simulate Prompting?" https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20989 . P. Llama-3.2-1B, MAML-style meta-training so that one gradient step on a context mimics prompting. Knowledge tasks, not rule prompts: SQuAD ~1/4 of prompted gap recovered, WikiText ~1/2; data-limited. Different problem (per-document), included for the 1B point.
30. Charakorn et al. (Sakana) 2026, Doc-to-LoRA. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15902 . P. Hypernetwork emits a LoRA per document (gemma-2-2b-it target; meta-training ~5 days on 8 H200). 82.5% of ICL ROUGE-L on SQuAD, sub-second. Useful number for 'how many generated queries does classic CD need': on 100 SQuAD items CD with 20/50/100 generated queries reaches 0.506/0.601/0.650 of ICL vs oracle-query CD 0.988 and D2L 0.866. Not useful for ONE fixed prompt (meta-training cost).
31. Hao et al. 2026 (Zhejiang/Tencent), LoCD. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21427 . P. Qwen3-8B/Falcon3-10B/Qwen3-Coder-30B (not small). Key mechanism: on-policy CD cannot learn behaviours the student rarely produces (effectiveness falls with student initial accuracy; answer-length delay in a knowledge-injection test: L=4 learned first, L=32 lags), while off-policy CD learns all lengths fast but has exposure bias. Fix: teacher regenerates only the critical span from the student prefix. Math avg 42.4 (OPCD) -> 48.0 (LoCD); 3 seeds.
32. Ma et al. 2026 (UW-Madison/JHU), PromptSD. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18293 . P. Qwen3-1.7B-Base and Phi-4-mini. Teacher = base + learnable soft prompt (NOT a text prompt), then OPD. Useful side data on 1.7B-Base: 3-shot ICL teacher: Science 35.3 (zero-shot 29.3), Tooluse 0.0 (zero-shot 7.2), Biology 38, Math 57.0 (16.4). SDFT-demo avg 34.0, OPSD(GT) 22.6, SDPO 14.9 vs base 21.2 (self-teachers with privileged text are weak at 1.7B). Divergence: reverse KL Math 51 vs forward 13 / JSD 14 / symmetric 18. 3 seeds for single-task OPD-PT (std up to 4.2). Biology n=50.
33. Armandpour et al. 2026 (Apple), "Unmasking On-Policy Distillation". https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10889 . P. Qwen3-0.6B and 1.7B students, BoolQ/MMLU (+4 AIME case studies). Training-free gradient-alignment diagnostic (NOT training outcomes). For 0.6B self-teachers with a correct solution in context beat 4B/8B/14B external teachers (2-3x alignment); for 1.7B external 8B teacher wins on BoolQ. Wrong demos hurt on easy tasks, help on hard math. Authors' reading: student must be able to comprehend the teacher signal. Distillation signal is better on the student's failing rollouts than on correct ones.
34. Harne et al. 2026 (Microsoft Research), "Privileged, but Biased". https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04794 . P. Qwen3-8B/32B. Lone self-distillation loss falls, accuracy flat or down on hard tasks (MMLU-Pro, DAPO-math, CodeForces, BFCL) while reproducing gains on easy SciKnowEval. PI Bias Score 0.52 on the shown solution vs <=0.02 on a different correct solution. 55.4% of loss on stopwords/punctuation/uncertainty markers. Hints and skills as PI also fail. Concerns instance-specific PI, not a fixed system prompt.
35. Zhao, Wu, Bau, Shi 2026, "Persona Hierarchy Model". https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09384 . P. Qwen3-4B, Llama-3.1-8B, 120 fine-tuned models. Behaviour fine-tuned under the DEFAULT context (no system prompt) spreads to all other system prompts; behaviour trained under a distant persona prompt stays local (r=0.72 Qwen3-4B). KL to the base model under the default prefix confines learning (cross-prefix transfer 39.4 -> 4.5 points). Implication: an internalised behaviour trained without any prompt is, by design, the global default.
36. Alfarra et al. 2026 (Qualcomm AI Research), soft-prompt distillation for on-device safety. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09388 . P. Llama-3-1B/3B, Qwen2-1.5B, Gemma2-2B, 4-bit, 100 soft-prompt vectors (still 100 tokens at inference). Teacher = base + LlamaGuard-1B filter. At matched parameter count soft prompts beat LoRA (rank 2-3) and steering vectors; plain perplexity training overfits and lowers safety/utility; KL distillation over-refuses (84.6% of safe prompts vs 36.1% for TV loss, 27.3% base). Included as small-model caution on loss choice; not text-prompt internalisation.
37. Padmanabhan et al. 2026 (Cornell), DiSC. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16093 . P. Knowledge adaptation of Qwen2.5-3B/7B, Llama3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B by context distillation over split document contexts; fine-tuning (even +KL/+LoRA) forgets IFEval/Math/Code, CD forgets less. Supports 'CD forgets less' claim; knowledge not rule prompts.
38. Tian et al. 2026, "What should a self-teacher see?" https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25623 . P. Qwen3 1.7B/4B/8B math OPSD: compact abstractions (category/framing, 18-39 tokens) beat full solution by 1.4-1.6 points at 4B/8B; answer-only within 0.2 points at 4B/8B but 1.76 worse at 1.7B. Initial teacher-student KL does not order utility. 3 seeds only for some arms.
39. Zou et al. 2026 (Scale AI), PI effectiveness in OPSD. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22512 . P. 4B/30B agents; error-step KL contrast predicts post-training performance (rho 0.10-0.94); PI 'utility' to the teacher is distorted by leakage. Offline screening idea.
40. Zhang et al. 2026, 'Post-training leaves behavioral shadows' (ATD). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29233 . P. Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B etc.: capabilities leak through single-word outputs on unrelated prompts (+5.3 pp HumanEval+). Tangential; only relevant as a hint that weight updates leave traces in unrelated behaviour (audit/leakage). Not used for claims.

## F. Added in round 3 (2026-11-08; full text read via alphaXiv PDF pages)

43. Hubotter et al. (ETH/MPI/MIT/Stanford) 2026, "Reinforcement Learning via Self-Distillation (SDPO)". https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20802 , code https://github.com/lasgroup/SDPO . P. Self-teacher = current policy conditioned on rich feedback (runtime errors, a successful sibling rollout); loss = per-token KL to stop-grad teacher (top-K=20-100 logits + tail term), EMA/trust-region teacher, JSD for stability; verl, 4 GH200. Qwen3-8B LCBv6 48.8 vs GRPO 41.2 (3 seeds). SCALE: gain over GRPO grows with size (Qwen3 0.6B->8B); SDPO UNDERPERFORMS GRPO on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct; at Qwen3-0.6B mixing GRPO advantages (lambda=0.9) is more robust. Forgetting: SFT on initial self-teacher outputs holdout avg 41.4 vs SDPO 42.4 vs base 43.5. Including the student's own attempt in teacher context biases teacher and cuts entropy. Authors: self-teaching is "emergent with scale". Not fixed-prompt internalisation, but the cleanest scale curve for self-teachers.
44. Zuin, Huet, Rossi, Ben Houidi (Huawei Paris) 2026, "Disentangling Self-Distillation: Acquisition and Retention". https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39494 . P. 1,200 runs, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Ministral-3-3B-Instruct, 5 tasks (2 "contradictory": rotated-compass spatial, base-9 arithmetic), 2 seeds, Wilcoxon + Holm. Axes: rollout source (student vs teacher), teacher coupling (frozen vs EMA), KL direction. Findings: (i) teacher rollouts beat student rollouts by median +8 pp acquisition on contradictory tasks (e.g. Qwen math-contra +12.7) with ~no retention change; little difference on ordinary tasks; (ii) EMA teacher helps up to a task/model-dependent rate then hurts (Ministral peaks at 0.05, loss at 0.25) - reconciles SDFT vs Kim et al.; (iii) KL direction matters least, retention cost model-dependent; (iv) SFT reaches the highest acquisition almost everywhere but loses 3-21 pp retention; most SD settings lie beyond SFT's Pareto front. Context = reference response (not a rule prompt).
45. Koh et al. (KAIST/LG AI Research) 2026, "Continually Post-training Small Models via Synthetic Self-Distillation (SynthSelf)". https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13113 . P. Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/3B-Instruct. SDFT acquisition relative to SFT falls monotonically with size (about -24 to -27 pp at 0.5B). At 0.5B self-rewrites are ~60% correct and drop CoT 8.2% of the time. Policy KL shift after fine-tuning correlates with forgetting (Spearman -0.85, 23 datasets, Qwen2.5-3B, LoRA). Fix: a slightly larger SAME-FAMILY model (Qwen2.5-7B/14B) rewrites demonstrations, rejection sampling on answer, then plain SFT. Avg acquisition 56.4 vs SFT 52.2 vs SDFT 44.1, retention 41.7 vs 40.5/41.5 (base 42.8). Same-family 7B aligner beat stronger cross-family Qwen3.5-27B/Gemma-4-31B (lower NLL under the student matters more than teacher accuracy). Single seed (42). Closest sub-1B outcome evidence for "teacher-generated text -> student SFT", though the context is a demonstration, not a rule prompt.
46. Blank, Bhatia, Rajamanoharan, Conmy, Nanda 2026, "Subliminal Learning Is Steering Vector Distillation". https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00995 (code github.com/agu18dec/steering-vector-distillation). 50. Zhang, Chow, Fang, Zhu, Liang, Chua (NUS) 2026, "What Does Privileged Information Add to On-Policy Self-Distillation?" https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20612 . P. 51. Li, Zuo, He et al. (Tsinghua) 2026, "Rethinking On-Policy Distillation: Phenomenology, Mechanism, and Recipe". https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13016 , code github.com/thunlp/OPD . P. Two conditions for OPD to work: (i) thinking-pattern consistency between student and teacher (measured by top-k token overlap at student-visited states), (ii) the teacher must carry something new; same-family 1.5B and 7B teachers are "distributionally indistinguishable" to the student. Successful runs: overlap ratio rises 72% -> 91%; shared top-k tokens hold 97-99% of mass; supervising only overlap tokens matches full top-k. Failing runs: stagnant overlap, small gradients, even though the failing teacher's reward is as correlated with correctness (AUROC 0.75 vs 0.73). Math only. Relevance: for self-distillation of a prompt, condition (i) is satisfied by construction and the prompt is the "new" thing; overlap ratio is a cheap monitor.
