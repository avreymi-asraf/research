## Round 2 additions

Round 2 findings on existing sources:
- [1] Askell, full text (sec 2.1, App. B.5): distillation data = 52B prompted model's top-50 log-probs (+1 "other" bucket) on a 50/50 mix of generic pretraining text and Stack Exchange, ~1500 tokens/sequence; ALL student sizes were trained from this one 52B teacher (so it is cross-size, off-policy, forward KL). Small models (<13B) scored worse WITH the prompt on alignment evals. Contractors preferred the fully prompted model over its distilled version ~53% of the time (weak evidence of small degradation). Fig 20: advantage of distillation over plain finetuning on the prompt shrinks once a short prompt is added after tuning.
- [3] Prompt Baking, full text: LoRA; trajectories sampled from the prompted model on QA-dataset questions (SQuAD for instruction prompts); instruction-following prompts baked to within 8% of prompted; few-shot CoT baked to within 1.4% on 6 benchmarks (3 seeds); re-prompting baked model: GSM8K 81.0 vs Llama-3 published 79.6 (the +1.4 is vs Meta's number, same few-shot examples); full-logit ablation on one task (reverse-input, SQuAD): a "critical mass" of logits is needed; "5 minutes" is a best case. Model size not located in extracted text. Contradicts [17] on re-prompting (see report).
- [5] OPCD, re-read appendix: checkpoints saved every 2 steps; system-prompt results = test accuracy averaged over the three best checkpoints; math/game runs "choose the checkpoint with highest test accuracy" => selection on the test set, optimistic. Reverse KL over the top-256 student-probability tokens; lr swept in [1e-6, 5e-6]; batch 128; no LoRA mentioned (full fine-tuning, per repo flags actor_lr 5e-6).
- [13] TRL: source of gkd_trainer.py (fetched via summarising tool, quotes plausible) confirms teacher and student get identical input_ids/attention_mask; gold_trainer.py shows no teacher-specific prompt either (first 100k of 164k chars read).

## Round 3 additions

Upgrades: [18] Many Faces: now FULL TEXT (sources/manyfaces_2605.11182.txt). Confirmed: system-prompt internalization = fixed prompt as PI for all questions; reasoning-compression (OPSD, Qwen3-8B) "requires a sufficiently capable model, such as an 8B-scale model"; safety (Qwen3-1.7B, WildGuardMix) OPSD rises fast, then is capped by the teacher while GRPO keeps gaining; stop-gradient or renormalised Top-K fixes collapse; 1.7B-Base student emitted garbled non-English output until teacher-trace SFT warm-up. [19] Tang & Yang: now read beyond the intro (sources/tangyang_2610.09639.txt). Natural-language test exists: Qwen3-4B-Base (after Tulu-3 SFT), Knowledge-2026 (809 post-cutoff QA): student 11.25 -> 9.64 after reverse-KL OPD from a teacher at 84.55; Math-2026 17.41 -> 23.88 from a teacher at 43.78. Caveat: the teachers are SFT-tuned models, not prompted copies, and "facts" are held in teacher weights, not a prompt.
